A changing station that works at 3am

At 3am you have one free hand and very little patience. A good changing station is one where everything you need is within reach of that one hand.

Keep it at arm’s length

Put wipes on the side of your free hand, not behind the baby. A flip-top pack you can open with one thumb saves more time than any gadget.

Two of everything

Keep a spare box of tissue on a low shelf and a second pack of wipes in the drawer. Running out halfway through a change is how 3am turns into 3:30.

Go gentle and unscented

Newborn skin reacts to more than you’d expect. Water-based wipes with no fragrance and unbleached tissue leave less behind on skin that’s still getting used to the world.
