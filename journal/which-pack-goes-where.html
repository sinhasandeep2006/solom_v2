Which pack goes where

Every SOLOMO format was designed for a particular spot in the house. Here’s the short version.

Bedside: the cube

Small footprint and pulls from the top, so you can reach it lying down.

Desk: the flat box

Low enough to slide under a monitor stand. It’s lint-free, so nothing ends up on your keyboard or glasses.

Bag and car: pocket packets

4-ply, so they still work after being squashed at the bottom of a bag for a month.
