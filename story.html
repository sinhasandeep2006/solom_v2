Our story

Everyday care, without the bleach.

SOLOMO ESSENTIALS began with a simple question at a bedside table: why does tissue have to be bleached white to feel clean?

It doesn’t. Bamboo fibre is long and strong on its own, so we skip the chlorine and keep the sheet the warm colour it naturally is. Everything else we make follows from that choice.

The material

100% bamboo pulp

Bamboo regrows quickly and its fibre is longer than most wood pulp. Longer fibres make a sheet that holds together when it’s damp and stays soft without heavy treatment.

The people

Made for families

Our formats come from real rooms: a cube for the bedside, a flat box for the desk, packets for the car door and a nursery pack for wherever a baby is likely to leak.

The box

No plastic windows

Cartons are recyclable card with nothing to peel off. The mascot trio on the sleeve, Orange Pebble, Blue Cube and Golden Star, is there so the box is worth keeping.
